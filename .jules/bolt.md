## 2024-05-02 - Tokenize optimization
**Learning:** Calling `llama_tokenize` twice to find out the exact length requires double the computation and two FFI boundary crossings.
**Action:** The common case is that tokenizing `text` requires slightly fewer or equivalent tokens than the number of characters in `text` (since words/subwords often group multiple chars into one token), plus maybe a few special tokens. Instead of calling `llama_tokenize` with a length of 0 to get the exact token count and then re-allocating exactly, we can guess the required capacity based on `text.len() + 4` (or similar heuristic), allocate an uninitialized vector, and do just *one* `llama_tokenize` call. If our guess was too small, it'll return a negative number (the negated required size), and *then* we resize and call again. This eliminates one FFI call and one tokenization pass in >99% of cases.
